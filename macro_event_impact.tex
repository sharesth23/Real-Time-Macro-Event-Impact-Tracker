\begin{abstract}
    Macroeconomic announcements are a primary source of discontinuous risk in financial markets.
    This paper presents a real-time event-study framework that quantifies the intraday impact
    of major macroeconomic releases on equities, foreign exchange, rates, and volatility.
    Using high-frequency market data aligned precisely with macro event timestamps,
    we measure post-announcement price reactions, volatility amplification, and shifts
    in cross-asset correlation structure.
    We find that high-impact macro events are associated with statistically significant
    correlation compression, indicating a temporary breakdown in diversification
    that has important implications for portfolio risk management.
    \end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Macroeconomic data releases such as inflation prints, employment reports, and central
bank rate decisions introduce concentrated information shocks into financial markets.
Unlike continuous trading risk, macro event risk manifests as abrupt repricing
across multiple asset classes within minutes of release.
Understanding both the directional impact and the systemic consequences of such events
is critical for traders, portfolio managers, and risk managers.

While traditional event studies focus primarily on average returns,
less attention has been paid to the dynamic behavior of cross-asset correlations
around macro announcements.
This paper addresses this gap by linking macro event risk directly to
correlation breakdowns, a key driver of systemic risk during stress periods.

\section{Data}

\subsection{Macroeconomic Events}
We study major U.S. macroeconomic announcements, including:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Consumer Price Index (CPI)
    \item Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)
    \item Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) rate decisions
\end{itemize}

Event timestamps and survey forecasts are obtained from a macroeconomic calendar API.
Each event is recorded with precise release times to ensure accurate alignment
with market data.

\subsection{Market Data}
Intraday market data at five-minute frequency is collected for representative
cross-asset instruments:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Equities: S\&P 500 index
    \item Foreign Exchange: EUR/USD, USD/JPY
    \item Rates: U.S. 10-year Treasury yield
    \item Volatility: VIX index
\end{itemize}

Prices are converted to log-returns for statistical analysis.
